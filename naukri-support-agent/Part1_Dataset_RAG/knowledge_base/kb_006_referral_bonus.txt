Existing employees are encouraged to refer qualified talent through the internal employee referral module on Naukri.com. 
Successful referrals for technical and managerial roles carry an incentive bonus of fifty thousand rupees, disbursed in two equal installments at three months and six months of the referee's active tenure. 
Referrals must be registered prior to the candidate's initial application submission, and members of the Talent Acquisition team and hiring managers are ineligible for bonus payouts for roles within their reporting hierarchy.
