All prospective hires must successfully undergo comprehensive background verification before their formal date of onboarding. 
The verification scope encompasses highest educational credential authentication, previous employment records for the past five years, criminal history clearance, and identity validation. 
Candidates are required to furnish self-attested documentation and authorized references within seven business days of accepting their offer letter. 
Any discrepancies or material misrepresentations identified during verification may lead to immediate withdrawal of the offer.
