Employment offers extended through the Naukri.com hiring network are finalized based on structured compensation bands determined by candidate experience, domain expertise, and internal equity standards. 
Candidates wishing to discuss or negotiate compensation components must submit formal counter-proposals with relevant documentary justification within five business days of offer issuance. 
Any revisions exceeding ten percent of the initial base salary offer require explicit written approval from the Head of Talent Acquisition and the Compensation Committee.
